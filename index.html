ASVAB Brain Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2026

ASVAB Brain is published by Zbyt Studios. It is an unofficial study aid and is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or sponsored by the U.S. Department of Defense or any branch of the U.S. military. ASVAB is a registered trademark of the Department of Defense.

Data we collect

None. ASVAB Brain does not collect, store on our servers, share, or sell any personal information. The app has no accounts, no advertising, no analytics, and no third-party tracking.

Data on your device

Your quiz progress, scores, streak, and rank are stored only on your device so the app can show your progress. This data never leaves your device. Deleting the app or using Reset Progress in the app erases it.

Internet access

The app works fully offline and does not need an internet connection.

Children

The app collects no information from anyone, including children.

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page.
